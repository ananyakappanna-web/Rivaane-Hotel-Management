The RIVAANE Legacy

Crafted for Those Who Appreciate the Extraordinary

RIVAANE is where centuries-old Indian hospitality meets contemporary design. Every corner tells a story, every stay becomes a memory.

Founded in 2024, RIVAANE emerged from a vision to create a sanctuary that honors the rich traditions of Indian palace hospitality while embracing the sophistication of modern luxury. Our name, derived from the Hindi word "Riwaayat" (tradition), reflects our commitment to preserving the essence of authentic Indian hospitality.

Each of our suites and rooms is designed to transport guests to an era of royal grandeur, complete with handcrafted furnishings, traditional artwork, and architectural elements inspired by India's magnificent palaces. Yet, beneath this heritage facade lies cutting-edge technology, world-class amenities, and sustainable practices that define modern luxury.
