FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

What is the check-in/check-out time?

Standard check-in is 2:00 PM and check-out is 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out can be arranged based on availability.

Do you offer airport transfers?

Yes, we provide luxury airport transfers. Please contact us at least 24 hours before your arrival to arrange this service.

Is parking available?

Complimentary valet parking is available for all guests. Our secure parking facility can accommodate all vehicle types.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, UPI, bank transfers, and cash. International cards are welcome.

Is the hotel pet-friendly?

We welcome well-behaved pets in select rooms. Please inform us at the time of booking. Additional charges may apply.

Do you have special packages for occasions?

Yes, we offer curated packages for anniversaries, honeymoons, birthdays, and corporate events. Contact us for customization.
